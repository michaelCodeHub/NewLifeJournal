2. What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, and your name and profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • A user ID created by our sign-in provider.

What you choose to record (health information)

  • Pregnancy: your name, due date, baby's name, hospital, doctor or midwife and their phone number; hospital visits (type, date, weight, blood pressure, notes, next appointment); symptoms and how strong they were; milestones; kick counts; contraction timings; your birth plan and baby checklist.
  • Baby: name, birth date, birth weight and length, gender (optional); feeds, diapers, sleep; growth measurements; vaccines, checkups, illnesses and medicines.
  • AI assistant: the questions you ask, the answers, and any photos or files you attach.
  • Community (if you use it): your posts, comments and likes, the display name and photo shown with them, and reports or blocks you make.

This information can reveal health details about you and your baby. We use it only to provide the app to you, and we ask for it only because you enter it.

Subscriptions

If you buy Bloom & Bump Plus, Apple or Google handles the payment. We receive your subscription status (for example "active until 3 June") through our subscription provider, never your card details.

Optional: usage and crash reports

Both are off unless you turn them on (You → Privacy).

  • Anonymous usage records which features are used (for example "a visit was added") so we can improve the app. It never includes health details, journal content or messages.
  • Crash reports include technical details about errors (device model, app version, where in the app it happened). They do not include journal content.

Stored only on your device

Your appearance and reminder settings, privacy choices, unsent forms and timers in progress are kept on your phone and are not sent to us. Reminders are scheduled by your phone.

3. How we use it

  • To run your journal: store it, sync it between your devices, show your week-by-week information, charts, timelines and reports.
  • To answer your questions in the AI assistant, personalised with your journal (see section 4).
  • To run the community, including moderation of reported content.
  • To provide Plus features you've paid for.
  • If you opted in: to understand usage and fix crashes.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, use it for profiling or targeted ads, or let advertising networks collect it.

4. The AI assistant

When you use Ask, your question and a summary of your journal (for example your pregnancy week or your baby's age, and recent symptoms, visits, feeds or sleep) are sent from our servers to our AI provider ([AI PROVIDER, e.g. Anthropic PBC]) to generate the answer. Photos you attach are sent too. Our AI provider processes this only to produce the answer and does not use it to train its models [CONFIRM WITH PROVIDER TERMS]. The assistant is not a doctor and does not give medical advice.

5. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the app, under contracts that limit them to doing so:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, database, file storage and servers; and, only if you opt in, usage analytics and crash reports. Data is stored in the United States.
  • Our AI provider (see section 4), only when you use Ask.

Other community members can see what you post in the community, with your display name and photo. Nothing else in your journal is visible to anyone but you. We may disclose information if required by law.

When you delete your account (You → Delete account), we permanently delete your profile, pregnancy and baby journals, AI conversations and attachments, community posts, comments and likes, and your sign-in.
